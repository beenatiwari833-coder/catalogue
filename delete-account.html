Delete Your TynTron Account

How to request deletion of your account and associated data from TynTron.

How to request deletion

TynTron does not yet offer in-app self-service deletion. To request that your account and associated data be deleted, email us directly:

info@tyntron.com

  1. Send an email to the address above from the email address registered on your TynTron account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  2. Include your registered mobile number (and business/vendor name, if you registered as a business) so we can locate your account.
  3. We'll verify your identity and confirm the request back to you by email.
  4. Your account and associated personal data will be deleted within 30 days of a verified request, except where we're required to retain certain information as described below.

What gets deleted

DELETED

  • Your profile information (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Your business listing and photos, if you registered as a service provider
  • Uploaded KYC documents (address proof, PAN, Aadhaar)
  • Saved addresses and society/location preferences
  • Account login credentials

What we retain, and why

RETAINED

  • Transaction and order records, where retention is required for accounting, tax, or legal compliance purposes
  • Records of past disputes, fraud reports, or policy violations, where needed to protect other users or meet legal obligations

This matches the retention terms described in our Privacy Policy (Section 12, Data Retention). Any retained data is kept only as long as legally necessary and is not used to provide you further services after your account is deleted.
